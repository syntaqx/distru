# Tasks

The **Tasks** page (titled "Calendar & Tasks") is where your team's work gets tracked. It has two views - a **Board** and a **Calendar** - over the same tasks, with a header showing totals for To do, In progress, Done, and Overdue.

## What a task carries
- **Title** and **description** - what needs doing.
- **Status** - **To do**, **In progress**, or **Done**.
- **Priority** - **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**, shown as a colored dot.
- **Due date** - drives the calendar and the overdue flag (a task that isn't Done and is past due shows in red).
- **Assignee** - a member of your workspace.
- **Linked record** - optionally tie a task to a **company** or an **order**, so it opens in context.

## The board
The Board is three columns by status. Cards show the priority dot, title, description, due date, assignee, and any linked record, and carry quick actions to **Start** (To do → In progress), **Complete** (In progress → Done), **Reopen** (Done → To do), edit, or delete. Cards are ordered by due date.

## The calendar
The **Calendar** view lays tasks out on a monthly grid by due date, with prev/next/Today navigation. Each day shows its tasks (with a "+N more" when a day is busy), and completed tasks are struck through. Tasks without a due date collect in a separate **No due date** list so nothing gets lost.

## Create and manage
Click **New task** to add one, or open a task to edit it. Status changes are one click from the board.

> The Copilot can manage tasks for you - *"add a high-priority task to call Green Leaf about their overdue invoice, due Friday"* - behind a single approval, and the same task tools are available over the MCP server.
